# Eco Terrarium

## Product truth

Eco Terrarium is a browser-based ecosystem sandbox and calming idle simulation. The player observes a living micro-ecosystem inside a glass vessel, then adjusts sunlight, moisture, temperature, nutrients, time, and direct interactions to keep the food web in balance.

## Primary users

- Casual players who want a quiet, tactile nature simulation.
- Strategy and simulation players who want to observe evolutionary and population dynamics.
- Hackathon reviewers who need to understand the game loop quickly.

## Core user jobs

- See the current ecology at a glance.
- Change an environmental condition and understand its likely effect.
- Inspect a specific organism and track discoveries.
- Preserve or share an interesting ecosystem state.

## Product constraints

- Preserve the existing simulation, audio, species, quests, and DNA-sharing behavior.
- The canvas is the primary experience. UI should assist observation rather than compete with it.
- The application must work from a 320px mobile viewport through desktop screens and respect browser autoplay constraints.
